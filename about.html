Jurleo's 30th

About

From the 24th to the 26th of July, 30 hours of partying, celebration and fun as we gather to celebrate our favourite baddie — Jurleo's 30th!

For Jurleo, the best birthday gifts are those shared by friends. Expect fun activities hosted by friends of Jurleo throughout the day in & around the villa. For a closer look at the schedule, head to the programme tab.

Good to know:
Feel free to drop by anytime across the two dates from door open.

Just coming on Saturday?
Do bring along:

  • A spare change of clothes, toiletries, and towels — there will be showers available.
  • A sleeping bag or cot — you're welcome to crash, even if you don't have a bed.

Staying over?
Just bring your clothes and toiletries (although shampoo and body wash are provided)!
